# 🧭 Global Cyber Policy roadmap

One consolidated view of proposed guidance, learning, policy coordination, and
related GitHub work. **Review draft · Sources checked 20 November 2026.**
Horizons and potential owners are suggestions, not WG commitments or assignments.
Published for review in a personal fork, not as an official WG roadmap.
No live Project exists for this pilot; upstream adoption requires WG review.

**Horizon:** 🟢 **Now** = suggested first focus · 🟡 **Next** = following priorities · 🔵 **Future** = backlog, not a promise.
**Status:** 💡 Proposal · 🔁 Recurring/maintenance · 📝 Source reports delivery; publication/completion not independently verified.
**Owners:** Awareness = GCP Awareness SIG; Main WG includes former Standards SIG work.
Forums are potential coordinating leads; individual coordinators are **Unassigned** unless an existing assignee is explicitly noted. APAC and Office Hours can contribute feedback without becoming assigned owners.
**Links:** 💬 open issue · 🛠️ open PR · 🔀 merged PR. Links may cover only part of a row or related work, not the whole deliverable.
